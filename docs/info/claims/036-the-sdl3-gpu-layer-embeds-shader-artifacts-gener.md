## Claim

The SDL3 GPU layer embeds shader artifacts generated from owned HLSL for SPIR-V, DXIL, and MSL, selects the active backend format, and executes a full-target graphics pipeline whose 48-pixel readback passes while its wrong-color control rejects 48 of 48 pixels

## What would falsify it
